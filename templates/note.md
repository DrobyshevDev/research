# The claim in one line, as the paper would put it

One paragraph a reader can stop at. What was asked, what was done, what came
out. No numbers yet.

## What the paper claims

The claim as its authors state it, in their terms, without correction. Getting
this right before disagreeing is the work.

## What the evidence shows

The measurement, the comparison, the size of the effect, the number of subjects
or seeds. This is where the claim and the evidence are allowed to differ, and
where the difference gets named.

## What would change the reading

The result that would overturn this, or the control that is missing. A note
that cannot say what would falsify it is describing a belief.
